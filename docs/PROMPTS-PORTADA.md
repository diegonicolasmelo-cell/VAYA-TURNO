# Prompts de portada — pantalla de inicio

Tres escenas para la pantalla de inicio de *¡Vaya Turno!*. Cada prompt es
**completo y autónomo**: lleva adentro el estilo de la casa (el mismo de las
115 cartas de `PROMPTS-ARTE.md`) y una regla de encuadre propia de la
portada — la acción en el 55% de arriba y el 40% de abajo vacío a propósito,
que es donde caen el logo y los botones del menú.

Formato: **3:4 vertical** (o 9:16 si Flow lo ofrece — calza exacto con la
pantalla del teléfono). Nunca cuadrado, nunca horizontal.

---

## A · La calma antes del turno  🥇 recomendada

```
Modern flat-vector cartoon illustration in the style of a contemporary
animated TV series. Clean digital finish: smooth flat color fills with soft
two-step cel shading plus gentle airbrushed gradients on skin and fabric.
No visible brush texture, no grain, no photorealism.

LINE: medium-weight outline in desaturated dark brown or deep teal, never
pure black; even and confident, slightly tapered at the ends. Interior
detail lines noticeably thinner than the silhouette.

CHARACTERS: adult naturalistic proportions with a slightly enlarged head
(about 5 to 6 heads tall). Large round eyes with clear white sclera and dark
irises; thick expressive eyebrows doing most of the acting; simple mouths,
soft rounded jawlines. Chilean public-hospital cast: varied ages, builds and
skin tones. Ordinary hospital clothing: teal or navy scrubs, white coats,
lanyards.

SCENE: the corridor of a hospital intensive-care unit, twenty minutes before
the shift starts. In the foreground, seen from behind at a three-quarter
angle, a cleaning auxiliary in a lime-green work uniform and rubber clogs
slowly mops the polished floor — shoulders relaxed, one earphone in, plainly
humming along to something. A small transistor radio and a spray bottle ride
on the yellow janitor cart; a folded yellow WET FLOOR cone stands alongside.
The damp floor holds a soft mirror reflection of the ceiling lights. Behind,
the corridor opens onto three glass-walled ICU rooms: beds freshly made with
the sheets still creased flat, monitors dark, ventilators parked and idle,
one IV pole standing alone. Far down the corridor, three tiny background
figures get ready without hurry — one stocking a supply cart, one writing on
a magnetic whiteboard, one carrying two coffees. A window at the far end
lets in low early-morning light.

MOOD: peaceful, unhurried, almost tender. Nobody is tired and nobody is
worried — this is the last quiet moment of the day. Do NOT add generic
tiredness, eye bags or worry to any face.

COLOR: the ENTIRE image is graded into ONE dominant hue — clean pale
hospital celeste-teal across floor, walls, glass and scrubs — with a narrow
value range and low overall contrast. Exactly two accent colors: the warm
cream-gold of the morning light at the end of the corridor, and the single
yellow of the janitor cart and cone.

LIGHT: soft ambient overhead light, gentle falloff, no hard shadows, no rim
light, no lens flare.

DEPTH: strong one-point perspective down the corridor. The foreground
auxiliary is the one clear silhouette; the rooms behind sit one step lower
in contrast.

TONE: affectionate workplace comedy — competent people, warm humour, never
cruel and never grim.

TITLE-SCREEN LAYOUT (important): portrait 3:4. All the action, the figures
and the identifying architecture sit in the UPPER 55% of the frame. The
LOWER 40% is deliberately empty and quiet — bare polished floor with only a
soft reflection, no figures, no props, no detail — because the game's logo
and menu buttons are drawn on top of it. Keep that lower area even in tone
so text stays readable over it. Nothing important touches any edge.

AVOID: photorealism, watercolor, painterly texture, thick black comic-book
inking, chibi or super-deformed proportions, neon or rainbow palettes, real
brand logos, and readable text of any kind.

Portrait orientation, aspect ratio 3:4 (or 9:16 if available) — never square
and never landscape.
```

**Variante noche** — cambia dos líneas: `A window at the far end lets in low
early-morning light` → `The corridor is lit only by low night lighting and
the blue glow of one screen at the nurses' station`; y el acento cálido pasa
a `the amber pool of the single desk lamp at the station`.

## B · Las puertas de la UCI

```
Modern flat-vector cartoon illustration in the style of a contemporary
animated TV series. Clean digital finish: smooth flat color fills with soft
two-step cel shading plus gentle airbrushed gradients. No visible brush
texture, no grain, no photorealism.

LINE: medium-weight outline in desaturated dark brown or deep teal, never
pure black; even and confident, slightly tapered at the ends. Interior
detail lines noticeably thinner than the silhouette.

SCENE: the double swing doors of a hospital intensive-care unit, seen
head-on from the corridor outside, filling the frame like a portal. Wide
institutional doors with big round porthole windows and scuffed metal kick
plates. Warm light spills through the portholes and through the gap under
the doors, drawing two long glowing strips across the floor. Above the
doors, a simple backlit sign reading only "UCI", and a small red lamp beside
it. On the wall alongside: an alcohol-gel dispenser, a card reader, and a
rack of folded yellow isolation gowns. An empty gurney is parked against the
left wall with a blanket folded on it. Nobody is in the shot, or at most one
small silhouette suggested through a porthole. The polished floor reflects
the doors faintly.

MOOD: anticipation and quiet gravity — a threshold about to be crossed.
Calm and inviting, never ominous, never horror.

COLOR: the ENTIRE image is graded into ONE dominant hue — cool hospital teal
across doors, walls and floor — with a narrow value range and low overall
contrast. Exactly two accent colors: the warm amber leaking from the
portholes and from under the doors, and the single red of the lamp.

LIGHT: soft ambient light, gentle falloff, no hard shadows, no lens flare.
The only bright source is the warm light behind the doors.

TONE: affectionate workplace comedy — the world behind these doors is run by
competent people. Warm, never grim, never a horror movie.

COMPOSITION: symmetrical and frontal, doors centred and occupying the upper
55% of the frame.

TITLE-SCREEN LAYOUT (important): portrait 3:4. The doors and everything
identifying sit in the UPPER 55% of the frame. The LOWER 40% is deliberately
empty and quiet — just the reflective polished floor and the two glowing
strips of light, no figures, no props — because the game's logo and menu
buttons are drawn on top of it. Keep that lower area even in tone so text
stays readable over it. Nothing important touches any edge.

TEXT: the ONLY letters allowed anywhere in the image are the three
characters "UCI" on the sign. No other writing, no labels, no signage.

AVOID: photorealism, watercolor, painterly texture, thick black comic-book
inking, neon or rainbow palettes, real brand logos, and any other text.

Portrait orientation, aspect ratio 3:4 (or 9:16 if available) — never square
and never landscape.
```

## C · La urgencia desde afuera

```
Modern flat-vector cartoon illustration in the style of a contemporary
animated TV series. Clean digital finish: smooth flat color fills with soft
two-step cel shading plus gentle airbrushed gradients on skin and fabric.
No visible brush texture, no grain, no photorealism.

LINE: medium-weight outline in desaturated dark brown or deep teal, never

CHARACTERS: adult naturalistic proportions with a slightly enlarged head
(about 5 to 6 heads tall). Large round eyes with clear white sclera and dark

Chilean public-hospital cast: varied ages, builds and skin tones. Scrubs,
high-visibility paramedic jackets, lanyards.

SCENE: the ambulance ramp of a Chilean public hospital's emergency
department, seen from across the driveway at dusk. Two ambulances at the
covered entrance: one has just backed in, rear doors open and roof lights
turning, and two paramedics in high-visibility jackets roll a stretcher down
the ramp; the second is arriving from the right with its headlights on.
Under the canopy, three hospital staff in scrubs come out to receive them —
one holding the sliding door, one pulling on gloves, one already talking to
the crew. A wheelchair and a spare gurney wait to one side. Above the
entrance, a large illuminated red cross. Light rain has just stopped: the
asphalt is wet and mirrors the red and amber lights in long vertical smears.
Behind the hospital, the low silhouette of the building against a dim
evening sky.

MOOD: urgent but professional — momentum and teamwork, everyone knows their
job. Energetic, never panicked. No blood, no visible injuries; the person on
the stretcher is fully covered by a blanket.

COLOR: the ENTIRE image is graded into ONE dominant hue — deep nocturnal
slate-blue across sky, building and wet asphalt — with a narrow value range.
Exactly two accent colors: the red of the emergency lights and the cross,
and the warm amber of the canopy lighting.

LIGHT: soft ambient dusk light with two warm practical sources (the canopy
and the headlights). No hard shadows, no lens flare, no light rays.

TONE: affectionate workplace comedy — competent people at the top of their
game, warm humour, never cruel and never grim. This is a comedy about a
hospital, not a medical drama.

COMPOSITION: a slight low three-quarter angle so the ambulances read big.

TITLE-SCREEN LAYOUT (important): portrait 3:4. The entrance, the ambulances
and the figures sit in the UPPER 55% of the frame. The LOWER 40% is
deliberately empty and quiet — the wet empty asphalt of the driveway
carrying only soft colored reflections, no figures, no props, no detail —
because the game's logo and menu buttons are drawn on top of it. Keep that
lower area even in tone so text stays readable over it. Nothing important
touches any edge.

AVOID: photorealism, watercolor, painterly texture, thick black comic-book
inking, chibi proportions, neon or rainbow palettes, real brand logos, blood
or injury, and readable text of any kind.

Portrait orientation, aspect ratio 3:4 (or 9:16 if available) — never square
and never landscape.
```

## Cómo se monta después

La imagen entra como fondo de `pantallaInicio()` en
`tools/app-plantilla.html`, a sangre completa y con un degradado al color de
papel en el tercio inferior para que el menú se lea. Si el 40% de abajo sale
con detalle igual, se recorta la banda superior y listo — es el mismo truco
que ya usan las cartas.
